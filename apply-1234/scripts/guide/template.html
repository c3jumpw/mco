Hiring team guide

How apply1234 works.

apply1234 turns your notes on a role into a job posting with a hidden link. Candidates who find the link answer your questions on video. You then review, score and interview them in one place. This guide shows every screen, menu and setting, with pictures.

Hiring leadSet up and post a jobNotes, AI draft, the hidden link, activity steps for the role, and job boards. ReviewerReview candidatesGo through answers, score them, read the signals, shortlist and interview. CandidateSee what applicants seeEvery screen of the application, from the posting to the thank-you page.

Everyone on the hiring team has the same access, so every part of this guide applies to every team member. Pictures use sample data. Updated November 8, 2026.

01 · The big picture

From notes to a hire

Three stages. You set the job up, candidates apply through the hidden link, and you decide. The app measures how each candidate moves through the middle stage.

05 · Hiring lead

Activity steps

An activity is a short list of steps. Each step has a type, and each type tests something different. Pick the ones that fit the role: a marketing lead might critique your homepage and send a one-page plan, a technician might answer two must-haves and show photos of past jobs. Video is one option, not the only one.

The Steps tab

  • + Add a step opens the library of all ten types. Click a step to open its settings: the question, a tip, and the options for that type, such as time limits, word guides, pasting, the right answer for a quick scenario, or what the brief sends back.
  • Presets by role loads a ready-made set of steps for one of ten kinds of role. Words in [square brackets] are for you or the AI to fill in.
  • Write with AI copies a prompt that fills in every step from your notes and posting, keeping each step's type. Paste the reply back with Paste the AI's reply.
  • The top line shows how long the activity takes. Past about an hour, many strong candidates drop off, and you'll see a warning.
  • Mark a step Optional to let people skip it. Right answers and must-have answers are never sent to candidates; answers are checked when they submit.

AI and honest answers

No tool can reliably tell AI writing from human writing, so the app doesn't try. Instead it makes honest answers the easy path and gives you the evidence to ask about the rest.

  • Ask about your material and their experience. AI doesn't know your homepage, your numbers or what they did at their last job.
  • Typed or pasted. Written answers record how much was typed and how much was pasted, how long they took and how often the person left the page. Candidates are told this. Per step, pasting can be allowed, allowed and noted (the default), or blocked on timed steps.
  • Timed scenarios leave no time to look things up. The clock keeps running if they reload.
  • Walkthrough videos on briefs: people who did the work explain it easily.
  • The AI question. On the Activity page tab, choose what candidates read: "AI is welcome. Tell us how you used it." (the default), a request not to use it, or nothing. Before submitting, each candidate says whether they used AI and how. You can also ask them to tick a box confirming the work is their own.
  • Follow-ups. On a candidate's page, the Interview card can copy a prompt that writes follow-up questions from their own answers. They're easy for the person who did the work and hard for anyone who didn't.

Further down the page

  • Résumé and cover letter appear when the candidate added them. Files open in a new tab, and the link expires after 10 minutes.
  • Notes are shared with the whole hiring team.
  • Tags are free labels like "strong writer", searchable on the Candidates page.
  • AI use, in their words shows whether they said they used AI, and how.

Export for AI review

The Export button builds one document with the role, steps, rubric and the candidate's answers, signals, scores and notes, with instructions for an AI reviewer at the top. Paste it into Claude or another model to get a second opinion. Switch on Hide names and emails for a blind review.

To compare everyone who applied to a job, use Export candidates on the job's Overview tab.

09 · Everyone

Inbox and help messages

Every activity page has a Help button in the corner. Candidates choose Something isn't working, Question about the role or Something else, write a message and leave their email.

  • New messages show an orange dot and raise the count on the Inbox menu item. Team members with alerts on also get an email.
  • Open a message to see the step they were on, their browser and screen size, any error they saw and whether they've applied. Yes, open their application jumps to their page.
  • Reply by email opens your email app with their message quoted.
  • Mark done moves it to the Done list. Add a private note for the team at any point.

Rules candidates work within

  • Progress is saved on their device, so they can leave and come back.
  • Every step that isn't optional needs an answer before they can continue. When transcripts are on, each video needs its transcript too. A box under the button lists exactly what is still missing.
  • A quick scenario can be answered once. If time runs out, it locks with whatever they picked.
  • Video length is a guide only and never stops them from submitting.
  • They can submit again to fix something. You'll see the second submission marked Repeat.

What they never see

  • The code word check, the signals and any of the tracking, apart from a note that typed and pasted text is recorded.
  • The right answers to quick scenarios and the must-have answers you need.
  • Other jobs. The site never lists jobs, and a job's page opens only through its own link.
  • This guide. It lives behind the admin sign-in.

15 · Everyone

Tips and fixes

How do I test a job the way a candidate would?

Open the job's activity link while signed in. Everything works, including on Draft jobs, and the page shows a "Preview" bar. Your visits and submissions are marked Test, left out of the dashboard, and their emails say [Test]. To see it exactly as a stranger does, use a private browser window.

A candidate says the link doesn't work

Check that the job is Live and, if they used a job board link, that the board link is switched on under Hidden link. Then open Activity › Wrong links: you'll see exactly what they typed.

A video shows no length

Loom doesn't report the length of some videos, often ones uploaded rather than recorded in Loom. The link still counts as valid and the candidate can submit. Watch the video to judge its length.

Can I change the link after posting?

Yes, but anyone who already pieced together the old link will hit "Nothing here". To retire one job board's link only, use New code or Turn off on that board instead.

How do I stop new applications?

Set the job's status to Closed on its Overview tab, or set a deadline on the Activity page tab. Visitors then see that applications are closed.

Someone applied twice

The newer submission carries a Repeat flag. Review the latest one and reject or delete the older one.

Emails aren't arriving

Check Settings › Email me when, then your spam folder. Candidate confirmations can be switched off per job on the Interview tab, so check that too.

Deleting a candidate

Delete candidate at the bottom of their page removes their answers, scores and notes for good. Moving them to Rejected keeps the record instead.

Use apply1234 like an app on your phone

On iPhone, open apply1234.com/admin in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app. It opens straight to your dashboard.
