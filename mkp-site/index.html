The story

Deconstructing things since I could pick up a device.

My family is from Sierra Leone, and I have spent most of my life in Atlanta. What started as a fascination with technology became a career as a freelancer, and it never lost the original habit: taking things apart to see how they work. First code, then whole businesses.

Building websites meant learning design, so I taught myself that too, then copywriting, then systems. What I ended up with is an unusual mix. The creative designer, the technician and the marketer in one person, which meant I could carry a client from a new look all the way through to what that look actually earned them.

The rest was learned the expensive way. A fifty dollar website sold door to door. A six hundred dollar check that changed the trajectory. A detour that cost me nearly everything, and the years spent rebuilding after it.
