The story

Deconstructing things since I could pick up a device.

My family is from Sierra Leone, West Africa, and I have spent most of my life in Atlanta. I started as a web developer and never lost the habit of taking things apart to see how they work. First code, then whole businesses.

That curiosity grew into business development, marketing, customer psychology and technology, learned mostly outside a classroom. Hundreds of business books, thousands of documents, people hired and rehired, cold calling, door to door, every bit of it. The first business was a clothing brand selling to college students. That became freelancing, then an agency, then clients ranging from startups to celebrities.

What I kept noticing is that I sat in an unusual spot: fluent enough in the technical side to build it, and fluent enough in the human side to explain it to the people in the boardroom and the people actually selling. That bridge turned out to be the whole job.

These days I split my time between serving clients and planting seeds for next year, building ventures, shipping software, and supporting communities overseas.

True success is sitting in the shade of a tree someone planted years before, and making sure you are planting those trees for other people too.
