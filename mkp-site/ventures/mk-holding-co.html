Holding company

The holding company behind the rest.

MK Holding Co partners with and invests in organizations across technology, consulting, real estate, consumer goods and industrial services, bringing capital, expertise and hands-on management to companies with a scalable path.

Visit MK Holding Co Get in touch

More than funding.

Portfolio companies get access to seasoned operators, strong governance structures and the kind of institutional support that usually only arrives with scale, without losing the speed that makes a smaller business competitive.

It is a long-horizon business. The point is acquiring and developing companies that create genuine value for their customers, their people and their markets, rather than optimising for a quick resale.

MK Holding Co sits behind the consulting, the investments and the software, and holds the standards all of them operate to.

How it operates

Consistent systems and practices

Repeatable playbooks and shared infrastructure, so every company it backs operates with the same clarity.

Continuous staff development

Leadership development, coaching and real pathways for the teams doing the work.

Relationships built on mutual benefit

Partnerships measured in decades rather than quarters, grounded in transparency and shared upside.

Sector breadth

Technology, consulting, real estate, consumer goods and industrial services, and more where the fit is right.
