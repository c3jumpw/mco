Browser app

One pile in. The right lists out.

HatRack is a sorting table for everything you have already written down. Paste in the lists from notes apps, emails and notebooks, hang each item on the business, project or part of life it belongs to, then hand the finished lists to the task app you actually work in.

Request a demo All projects

The problem is not capture. It is that everything landed in one pile.

Anyone running several things at once ends up with the same mess: notes on a phone, a half list in an email to yourself, three pages in a notebook, and a task app that only ever held part of it. None of it is sorted, so none of it gets done in any deliberate order.

HatRack exists for the step nobody has a tool for, which is sorting that pile. Paste the whole thing in. Headings and keywords place most of it automatically. What is left goes through triage, one key press per item, until the unsorted column is empty.

Then it leaves. HatRack is not trying to be your task manager. It exports each sector to Todoist, Asana, a spreadsheet or a calendar, and gets out of the way. It runs in the browser and keeps everything on your device.

How it works

Capture and import

Quick add with shortcuts for group, priority and date, or paste a long list and open a text, markdown or CSV file. Headings and keywords sort most of it on the way in.

Triage and sort

Whatever did not place itself waits in Unsorted and goes through one at a time, a key press each. Then drag, nest subtasks, and set dates and durations on the board.

Review and export

Read it as a board, a table, a printable outline or a set of numbers, then export each sector to the app you work in.

What is in it

No logins, no permissions, and nothing kept off your device.

Four views of one rack

Board for sorting, List for editing in a table, Outline for a printable document, and Reports for the counts and hours.

Quick add shortcuts

Type the task and let the shortcuts fill the rest in, naming the group, the priority and the day without leaving the box.

Import a pile

Paste a long list or open a text, markdown or CSV file. Headings become groups and your own keywords route the rest.

Triage mode

Unsorted tasks come up one at a time with a count of what is left, so clearing the backlog is a short repeated action rather than a project.

Groups that nest

A parent for each business, organization or obligation, entities beneath it, and sub-branches below those. Counts and estimated hours roll up.

Tasks that nest

Subtasks, notes and subnotes fold away inside a card until you expand it, so the board stays readable while the detail stays attached.

Export where you work

Todoist, Asana, a spreadsheet, a calendar and more, one sector at a time rather than all or nothing.

A setup you can share

Groups, types, priorities, statuses, durations and custom fields are configured once and shared as a template, so a team sorts into the same structure.

Practical details

  • RunsIn the browser, with everything kept on your device. Installable on a phone.
