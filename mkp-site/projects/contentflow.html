Strategy and production in the same place.

Content work usually splits in half. The strategy lives in a deck nobody opens and the production lives in a task manager with no memory of why any of it was planned.

ContentFlow keeps both on one board. Every account has a strategic home base covering voice, audience, goals and social profiles, and every piece of content moves through drafting, review and approval against it.

Teams see across every account they manage. Clients get a focused view of their own content, and anyone running several brands can switch between them from one login, merged into a single view by default.

What it covers

From the first idea to the published post, on one board.

Brand Foundation

A strategic home base for every account covering voice, audience, goals and social profile links for all major networks, with support for multiple strategy profiles per account.

Idea Capture

Anyone can jot down a content idea the moment it strikes, tagged to the content pillar it belongs to, so good ideas stop dying in direct messages.

Pillars and rhythm

Define what a brand posts about and how often. The pillars and the cadence together shape what gets suggested next.

Production Board

Track every piece through drafting, review and approval, in Board, List or Calendar view depending on whether you are working, scanning or scheduling.

Prompt Library

Reusable AI prompt templates that pull a brand's Foundation details in automatically, so every prompt is already on-voice instead of starting from a blank box.

Feed

A scrollable view of recent activity and published content, for catching up on what moved without opening every card.

How it runs

  • RolesAdmins and team members see across every account they manage. Clients see only their own content.
  • Multi-brandOne login can switch between accounts, merged into a single view by default.
  • SyncSyncs directly with a task management program so work is not entered twice.
  • AccountabilityA clear review and approval trail on every piece.
  • PlatformFully responsive, dark and light themes, and installs as a home-screen app with its own icon on desktop and iOS.
