The things you meant to come back to.

Most saved media never gets a second visit. Files pile up with no sense of what is in them and no reason to open any particular one today.

My PD attacks both problems. A Today's Practice card surfaces one audio file each day, so there is always an obvious place to start. Summaries on every item mean you can tell at a glance what something covers before committing twenty minutes to it.

Alongside the media sits a Vault: a personal knowledge base of terms, topics and ideas, each with its own follow-up question thread so you can keep digging into a concept without leaving the app.

What is inside

Browse and play

Navigate by folder in list or grid view with square cover art. The player supports variable speed and 15-second skip, and keeps playing in the background, including audio from video, when the screen locks.

Transcripts and summaries

Add timestamped transcripts by tapping along while something plays, or paste existing text with SRT and VTT supported. Every item can carry a summary, written by hand or generated from its transcript.

Collections

Group related items into tagged collections, each with its own custom header image. Built for a course, a theme, or a recurring practice series.

Vault

A personal knowledge base alongside your media, with a follow-up question thread on every entry.

  • Terms for quick vocabulary lookups with AI-assisted definitions and examples
  • Topics for deeper explainers that start simple and expand on request
  • Ideas for raw journal thoughts, paired with a generated summary and space to reflect

Organize

Rename, move and tag items in place. Deleted items go to Trash with a 30-day recovery window, with restore and confirm-to-delete built in.

Built for mobile

Large uploads are chunked and resumable, so they survive a flaky connection. Streaming playback means large videos start instantly instead of downloading in full. Dark and light themes, pull-to-refresh and an optional passcode lock.
