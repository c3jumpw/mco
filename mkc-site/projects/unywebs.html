Choice is the problem, not the solution.

Anyone starting a business is handed an endless catalogue of software, most of it reviewed by people who never ran that kind of business. The result is weeks lost to comparison and a stack that does not fit together.

Unywebs cuts the catalogue down. It curates the tools that actually work for small and growing businesses, explains what each one is for in a sentence, and handles setup where setup is the part that stops people.

The aim is simple: make technology less frustrating, so the business can move.
