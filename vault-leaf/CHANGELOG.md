# Vaultleaf change log

Every change to Vaultleaf, oldest first. Generated by `tools/build.py` from `src/changelog.json` (written entries) and
`src/changelog-auto.json` (logged automatically by the docs workflow). Edit those, not this file. Times are US Eastern.

## 2026-10-30

- **Project set up** (New, admins, v1.0) Vaultleaf's folder was created in the c3jumpw/public repository with a new Vercel project, ready for the first release. `3bfd6db`
- **Vaultleaf launches** (New, everyone, v1.0) An encrypted password vault that unlocks with your master password, then a passcode. A recovery key gets you back in if you forget them. Everything is encrypted on your device before it's saved. `ef5516c`
- **Classic notes view and Modern cards** (New, everyone, v1.0) Read accounts like the notes you keep passwords in now, one detail per line, or as cards with copy buttons. Write an account as a plain note and Vaultleaf turns the lines into fields. `ef5516c`
- **Everything an account needs** (New, everyone, v1.0) Passwords, PINs, API keys, secret phrases, 2FA codes, security questions with hidden answers, the account's admin users and their emails, the tool and account name, the date stored and notes. `ef5516c`
- **History and copy records** (New, everyone, v1.0) Each account keeps every change, the old passwords and keys it replaced, and the date and time of every copy. `ef5516c`
- **Labels, sub-labels and search** (New, everyone, v1.0) Group accounts with labels like folders, nest them as sub-labels, and search names, usernames, emails, websites, labels and notes. `ef5516c`
- **Import from Notes or a CSV file** (New, everyone, v1.0) Paste your notes, many at once, or bring a CSV export from a browser or another password manager. `ef5516c`
- **Generator, Health and Activity** (New, everyone, v1.0) Make strong passwords and passphrases, see weak, reused and overdue passwords, and review every copy and change across all accounts. `ef5516c`
- **Backups, restore and Recently deleted** (New, everyone, v1.0) Save an encrypted backup file and restore it on any device. Deleted accounts stay in Recently deleted for 30 days. `ef5516c`
- **Illustrated guide** (New, everyone, v1.0) A guide with pictures of every screen, inside Vaultleaf and on its own page. `ef5516c`
- **Published on Vercel** (New, admins, v1.0) Live at public-vault-leaf.vercel.app with strict security headers, offline use and home-screen install. Only people signed in to the Vercel team could open it. `ef5516c`

## 2026-11-08

- **Moved to the c3jumpw/mco repository** (Changed, admins, v1.1) The code moved to the vault-leaf folder of c3jumpw/mco. The Vercel project was renamed mco-vault-leaf and the address became mco-vault-leaf.vercel.app. Vaults saved at the earlier address stayed there. `fc0bd81`
- **Vaultleaf: note the move in the README** (Maintenance, admins, automatic) `b55b607`
- **Sync between devices** (New, everyone, v1.1) Turn on sync and enter your sync code on your other devices. Only encrypted data is uploaded, and edits on two devices are combined. `d20683d`
- **Sync storage in Supabase** (New, admins, v1.1) Synced vaults are stored encrypted in the MKC-Admin Supabase project, in the vaultleaf_vaults and vaultleaf_blobs tables. `d20683d`
- **Face ID and fingerprint unlock** (New, everyone, v1.1) Turn it on in Settings to unlock without typing. Your password and passcode are still asked for every 14 days. `d20683d`
- **Leaked password check** (Security, everyone, v1.1) Health can check your passwords against public breach lists. Only 5 characters of a scrambled fingerprint are sent, never the password. `d20683d`
- **Attach files** (New, everyone, v1.1) Keep ID scans, recovery sheets and short PDFs on the account they belong to, encrypted, up to 5 MB each. `d20683d`
- **Emergency kits** (New, everyone, v1.1) Make a file with everything, or only some labels, that a trusted person opens with its own code. `d20683d`

## 2026-11-20

- **What's new after each update** (New, everyone, v1.3) The first time you unlock after an update, you see what changed, with pictures. You only see changes that apply to you.
- **Choose your role** (New, everyone, v1.3) In Settings, say whether you run the Vaultleaf site. Admins get the Admin page and hear about admin changes. It doesn't open anyone else's vault.
- **Admin page with the change log** (New, admins, v1.3) Every change since launch, oldest first, with filters and downloads as JSON, CSV or Markdown. Find it under Admin.
- **The change log keeps itself up to date** (New, admins, v1.3) Every push that changes Vaultleaf is logged automatically. Changes people should hear about get a written entry, which also shows in their What's new.
- **New address: vaultleaf.mkcworkspace.com** (Changed, everyone, v1.3) Vaultleaf is moving to vaultleaf.mkcworkspace.com. Your vault doesn't move by itself. When the new address is ready, a banner here walks you through bringing it over with your sync code or a backup.
- **Who can open the new address** (Security, admins, v1.3) Vercel Authentication covers the vercel.app addresses only. vaultleaf.mkcworkspace.com opens for anyone with the link, and each visitor gets their own empty, private vault. To require Vercel sign-in there too, change Deployment Protection to cover all deployments.
